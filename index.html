How to use Valence

  • Draw a molecule in the sketcher (left panel)
  • Click Render Molecule or press Enter
  • Hover orbitals to see their type (σ, π, lone pair)
  • Rotate the view by dragging, zoom with scroll
  • Press R to reset the view
  • Try the Examples dropdown in the Build tab for pre-built molecules
  • Use the Style tab to change colors and materials
  • Use Measure tab to click atoms and measure distances/angles/dihedrals
  • Export as PNG, SDF, XYZ, or copy the SMILES
