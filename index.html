How to use Valence

  • Draw a molecule in the sketcher (left panel)
  • Click Build 3D model or press Enter
  • Hover orbitals to see their type (σ, π, lone pair)
  • Rotate the view by dragging, zoom with scroll
  • Press R to reset the view
  • Pick a model along the bottom (VSEPR, Charge Model, Localized, Delocalized): the panel on the right shows that model's controls
  • Press O to fold the model panel to the right edge, B to fold the sketcher to the left
  • Load a ready-made molecule from Load an example… above the sketcher, or press 1–9
  • The settings button (top right) holds the theme, structure options, display, measuring and export
  • Right-click the molecule to copy it as XYZ, SDF, or PNG
